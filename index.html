How it works

Six questions, one score.

6Six questionsFootprint, lock-in, self-direction, power, longevity and delivery, like a heuristic evaluation of how software treats its people. 30Scored out of 30Each tool gets 0 to 5 on every question and a verdict from Convivial to Restrictive. Scores are dated and open to change. 0Funded by readersReader tips keep it running, with no ads. +Make software?A free field guide shows which changes raise a score, and what they do to your income.
