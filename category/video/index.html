About these tools

Video sites choose what plays next and watch what you watch. VLC plays almost any file, mpv does it with a bare window, Kodi turns a computer into a media centre for your TV, Jellyfin streams your own films and shows from your own server, and NewPipe and FreeTube let you watch YouTube without an account or ads.

Most of these work best with video you own, and the YouTube viewers carry a risk: they depend on YouTube's pages, so they can break until their volunteers fix them.

How to choose

For one device, VLC is all most people need. If you have a library and a spare computer, Jellyfin or Kodi make it feel like a streaming service. To keep watching YouTube with less tracking, NewPipe on Android and FreeTube on the desktop are the closest fit.

Frequently asked questions

What is an alternative to YouTube?

NewPipe on Android and FreeTube on the desktop show YouTube videos with no account or ads, and PeerTube, in Social feeds, is a federated video site run by many hosts.

What plays every video file?

VLC and mpv play nearly every format with no account or ads.

Can I have my own Netflix?

Jellyfin and Kodi serve the films and shows you own to your TV and other devices.
